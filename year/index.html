Your year on PulseChain

Paste your wallet address. The page reads this year's transactions and your 1 January balances from the blockchain and makes a card you can save or share. The card shows percentages and counts only, never amounts.

Only add wallets that are yours: a wallet you only watch would count someone else's year as yours.
Reading the chain
Starting
A busy wallet can take a minute or two: every page of history comes from a public explorer.

What the card counts

A wallet had more history than the page reads (1,500 transactions or transfers per list), so counts marked + are lower bounds.

What is read. Transactions your wallets sent this year and token transfers in or out, from the public PulseChain explorers (FusionScan, then PulseScan); balances and HEX stakes on 1 January and now, from public PulseChain RPC nodes; today's prices from DexScreener. Both ends are valued at today's prices, so the stack change shows what you did, not how prices moved.

Nothing is stored or sent anywhere else. The page runs in your browser and only asks those public services. No account, no tracking, no server of ours involved.

Not counted in this web version: Ethereum (PulseChain only), the value of LP positions in the stack (new positions and liquidity adds are still counted), and .pls names (paste the 0x address). Coins with less than $1,000 of DEX liquidity are treated as spam.
