About

Ching Ching is the privacy-first portfolio tracker for PulseChain and Ethereum. No accounts, no sign-ups, no tracking: your wallets stay on your device.

Track multiple wallets by address, ENS name or .pls name. View token holdings with live prices, HEX stakes with T-Share analytics, NFTs, liquidity pool positions and on-chain stats, all updated in real time.

  • Multi-wallet support
  • HEX stake tracking
  • Treemap & pie charts
  • NFT gallery (ERC-404)
  • Home screen widgets
  • Custom widget token sets
  • Chain stats & gas prices
  • Gold ATH indicators
  • Wallet sparkline charts
  • Calendar export for stakes
  • Protocol staking (ICSA, COM, Liquid Loans)
  • PulseChain Oceanomics aquarium
  • Apple Watch companion
  • 17 languages
  • Dust token filtering
  • Transaction history
